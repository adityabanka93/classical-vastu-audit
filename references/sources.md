# Source register — classical Vāstu writings

**Status: research preview, 13 November 2026.** The links are bibliographic/reading pointers. Chapter numbers can differ by edition. This is not a completed critical edition, a complete Sanskrit corpus, or a scientific validation of traditional predictions.

## Primary works and useful editions

### 1. Varāhamihira, *Bṛhat Saṃhitā*, Vāstu-vidyā chapter

- English translation: N. Chidambaram Iyer (1884), Chapter 53:
  https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brihat-samhita/d/doc229297.html
- Sanskrit verse displays, check against an established edition:
  https://www.siva.sh/brihat-samhita/53/71-75
- Topics to verify against the text: ground-plan deity positions, marma or points to avoid, four sides and their doorway positions, nearby obstacles, door proportions, landscaping.
- The eight positions on a cardinal wall are **not** a universal license to cut any modern apartment wall into eight equal pieces and pronounce a deity. Check the actual plan scheme, source conventions, geometry, and whether the residence matches the described context.
- Traditional claims of health, prosperity, or hardship are documented *beliefs*; they are not demonstrated causal results.

**Important:** Chapter 38 describes doorway quarters associated with **all four** principal directions. Thus a modern consultant's blanket assertion that every south-facing or west-facing entry is prohibited cannot be attributed uncritically to the chapter. Building types, verses and edition matter.

- Supporting architectural text; obtain an accessible critical edition before treating any specific passage as verified.
- Secondary references can guide research but do not substitute for the primary passage.

## Citation quality requirements

For each finding note author/title, editor or translator, chapter, verse (if verified), exact concern, and caveats about alternative readings. Never invent Sanskrit quotations or exact chapter-and-verse attribution. Where the referenced edition has not been directly checked, mark the claim **needs source verification**.

## Anti-overclaim rule

No traditional doorway prediction establishes that a family's wealth or health will be harmed. An electronic 'energy meter' does not validate paranormal causes absent a clearly defined, reproducibly measured physical quantity and independent evidence.
