# Worked example — consultant claims one entrance can fix everything

**Fictional scenario:** A homeowner reports that a consultant says a south-facing apartment entry is 'wrong' and a paid metallic 'energy treatment' will correct it, claiming to use an electronic energy-scanning device.

This example contains no individual's floor plan or address.

## Review

| Claim | Source check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Every south-facing door is wrong | *Mānasāra* ch. 38 discusses doorway positions associated with all four directions, in specific settings. Exact verse/application must be checked. | **Not a valid universal statement** based on this chapter |
| The entry is in an inauspicious doorway division | Classical books prescribe different position schemes. No measured plan or trusted north direction supplied. | **Undetermined** |
| One doorway fix resolves all household issues | No passage is supplied demonstrating such a comprehensive cure. | **Unsupported by reviewed sources** |
| An electronic device objectively detects bad spiritual energy | No quantity, calibration, controls, reproducibility or independent physical validation supplied. | **Unverified scientific claim** |
| A paid remedy is essential | No specific source passage or practical necessity established. | **Not justified on current information** |

## Appropriate next step

Obtain a dimensioned, redacted plan with a credible north arrow and accurately identify whether 'entrance' refers to a property gate, common building entrance or unit door. Compare *Bṛhat Saṃhitā* and *Mānasāra* on their own terms; do not impose an unsupported generic grid. Separate actual safety issues from religious or traditional preferences.

**Reminder:** Even when a layout matches a traditional inauspicious position, that does not establish that it caused or will cause adversity.
